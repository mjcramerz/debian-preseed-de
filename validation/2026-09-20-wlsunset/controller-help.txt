usage: labwc-wlsunset [-h] [{start,stop,restart,status,check}]

Own one native colour daemon through a transient systemd user service. No
shell evaluation, fork-to-background, geolocation, polling daemon or X11. The
short-lived controller validates root-owned policy and the active compositor;
/usr/bin/wlsunset itself is the long-running, independently confined service.

positional arguments:
  {start,stop,restart,status,check}

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
